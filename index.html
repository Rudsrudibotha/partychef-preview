Our story

Cooking since he was 5 years old.

Greg Schmelz has been cooking since he was 5 years old and could barely reach the wood-burning cookstove in his mother’s farm kitchen. His first famous dish was fried potatoes, slightly crispy on the edges.

“Planning a party should be fun and not stressful. Leave the stress to the chef!”

Greg loves to entertain and cook for friends, so it seemed natural to start a business doing just that. The idea came up at a party over the summer of 2018. Already an accomplished cook, Greg attended Ivy Tech to obtain his Culinarian Certificate in 2019, as well as ServSafe and ManageFirst certifications.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Best to call us with details and get a more accurate answer.

What is the party size?

The average party is 8 to 10 people, and we’ll work with smaller groups as well. The maximum depends on the location, type of party and food — a sit-down steak dinner is likely limited to around 20 people, while hamburgers and hot dogs by the pool could be much larger. Call us with details for an accurate answer.

Is PartyChef insured?

Yes. We’re insured to cover any accidents that may occur in your kitchen as a result of PartyChef and our employees. Details of the policy can be discussed at the event planning meeting.

What about leftovers?

You’ve paid for the service and the food, so you keep the leftovers.

How long will you be at my house?

It depends on the meal and menu. Crème brûlée, for example, extends the prep time. On-site time is tailored to your party.

How do payments work?

We take 50% at booking and the remainder at the end of the party. We accept cash, personal checks and money orders.
